About the label

The Sound of the Group.

Wunderland Records is the flagship recording and publishing division of Wunderland Corporation, established in 2018 to house the group's most significant music assets.

The label was built on a philosophy of artist-first development — long-term creative partnerships, full ownership support, and a commitment to building careers rather than chasing chart cycles. Every artist on the Wunderland Records roster has been signed with a decade in mind.
