V's 'ABSENCE' Catalogue Acquired by Major Publishing House in Eight-Figure Deal

Wunderland Records' publishing arm has completed the licensing of V's ABSENCE catalogue to a major international publishing house in a deal reported to be in the eight-figure range. The agreement covers synchronisation, print, and neighbouring rights across 47 territories.
