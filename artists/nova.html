Extended profile · via wunderlandcorporation.com

NOVA is the most commercially successful artist on the Wunderland Records roster and one of the most compelling voices in contemporary R&B. Her music — a blend of classic soul architecture, modern production, and a vocal range that defies easy description — has earned her a global audience and a reputation as one of the defining artists of her generation.

Signed to Wunderland Records in 2020, NOVA's debut album under the label debuted at number three on the UK Albums Chart and produced three top-ten singles. Her second album went further, earning her a BRIT Award nomination and a headline slot at a major European festival.

Beyond the music, NOVA is a cultural figure — a voice on issues of identity, representation, and the politics of being a Black woman in the music industry. Her platform is as important as her catalogue.

“I was told I was too much. I decided that was the whole point.”

— NOVA
