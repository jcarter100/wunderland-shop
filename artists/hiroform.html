Extended profile · via wunderlandcorporation.com

HIROFORM is the most technically uncompromising artist in the Wunderland Records family — a producer and performer whose work sits at the intersection of industrial architecture, future-bass energy, and ambient precision. Their music is built, not written: engineered from the ground up with the same rigour as structural design.

Signed in 2022 after a series of releases on independent electronic labels that earned them a devoted following in the club and festival circuit, HIROFORM brought a different energy to Wunderland Records — colder, more precise, and more demanding of the listener.

HIROFORM's live performances are technical spectacles — custom-built hardware, reactive visuals, and a sound system specification that most venues struggle to meet. They are, by design, an acquired taste that becomes an obsession.

“Every sound has a shape. I just make the shapes visible.”

— HIROFORM
