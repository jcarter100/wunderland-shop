Extended profile · via wunderlandcorporation.com

BENTO is a genre-fluid force — an artist who moves between hip-hop, Afrobeats, R&B, and everything in between with an ease that makes categorisation feel redundant. Born from a multicultural background and raised on an eclectic diet of global music, BENTO's sound is a reflection of the world as it actually is.

Signed to Wunderland Records in 2021, BENTO brought an energy to the label that was immediately felt. Their debut project under the imprint debuted in the top 20 of multiple regional charts and earned them a reputation as one of the most exciting live acts in the UK urban scene.

BENTO's approach to music is inseparable from their approach to culture — every release is accompanied by a visual world, a community activation, and a statement of intent.

“Every song is a meal. I want people to leave satisfied but already hungry for the next one.”

— BENTO
