Extended profile · via wunderlandcorporation.com

WUNDERLAND — the artist, not the corporation — is the label's most surreal and otherworldly voice. A dream-pop visionary whose music exists in the space between waking and sleep, between memory and imagination, between the world as it is and the world as it could be.

Named in homage to the corporation that gave them a home, WUNDERLAND the artist embodies the label's founding philosophy: that art should disorient before it comforts, that beauty is most powerful when it is strange. Their music is a labyrinth — you enter willingly, and you are never quite sure of the way out.

Signed in 2024 following a series of self-released concept albums that built a devoted cult following, WUNDERLAND brings a theatrical, immersive quality to the label's roster that no other artist can replicate.

“Reality is just a story someone told you. I'm here to tell you a better one.”

— WUNDERLAND
