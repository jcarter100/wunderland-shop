V

“One letter. Infinite space.”

V is reduction as philosophy. A single letter, a single beam of light, and compositions stripped to their absolute essence. In December 2025, V's ABSENCE catalogue was acquired by a major international publishing house in an eight-figure deal covering synchronisation, print, and neighbouring rights across 47 territories — proof that silence, precisely arranged, commands a premium.

Extended profile · via wunderlandcorporation.com

V is an enigma by design. The artist known only by a single letter operates at the extreme edge of experimental pop — where melody dissolves into texture, where silence is as deliberate as sound, and where the listener is asked to bring as much as the artist gives.

Signed to Wunderland Records in 2022 after a series of anonymous releases that circulated through underground music communities, V's identity and origin remain intentionally obscured. What is known is the music: sparse, precise, and emotionally devastating.

V's debut album under Wunderland Records was released with no press campaign, no interviews, and no social media presence — and still found its way to over two million streams in its first month through word of mouth alone.

“I removed everything that wasn't necessary. What's left is everything.”

— V
