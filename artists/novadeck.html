NOVADECK

“Architect of sound. Navigator of stars.”

NOVADECK is the architect at the center of the Wunderland Records universe — a producer whose cosmic hip-hop instrumentals chart constellations rather than choruses. Following the critical and commercial success of STAR MAPS VOL. 2 and ORBIT, NOVADECK signed a multi-album extension with the label in May 2026, cementing one of the most productive producer-label relationships in the independent sector.

Extended profile · via wunderlandcorporation.com

NOVADECK is the producer's producer — a studio architect whose fingerprints can be found across some of the most significant releases in the Wunderland Records catalogue. As a solo artist, NOVADECK operates in the space between cosmic hip-hop, neo-soul, and instrumental composition.

A founding presence in the Wunderland Records creative ecosystem since 2019, NOVADECK has produced records for BENTO, VYSERION, and NOVA while simultaneously building a solo discography that has earned widespread critical respect. Their beat tapes are treated as standalone artistic statements.

NOVADECK's live show — a one-person orchestration of samples, synthesis, and live instrumentation — has been described as 'watching someone compose the universe in real time.'

“The best beat is the one that makes the artist feel like they've been waiting for it their whole life.”

— NOVADECK
