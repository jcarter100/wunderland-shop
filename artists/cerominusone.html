Extended profile · via wunderlandcorporation.com

CEROMINUSONE is Wunderland Records' most uncompromising sonic experiment — a project built entirely on the principle of subtraction. Where other artists add layers, CEROMINUSONE removes them, leaving behind only the essential frequency, the irreducible signal, the sound that cannot be cut without silence.

Operating at the intersection of noise art, industrial composition, and dark ambient, CEROMINUSONE challenges the listener's definition of music itself. Their releases are not albums in the traditional sense — they are documents of a process, evidence of a system being dismantled and rebuilt in real time.

Signed to Wunderland Records in 2023 after a series of limited-run physical releases that circulated exclusively through underground art spaces and noise collectives, CEROMINUSONE represents the label's commitment to art that refuses to be categorised.

“The most honest sound is the one that remains when you've removed everything you were told to keep.”

— CEROMINUSONE
