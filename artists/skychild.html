Extended profile · via wunderlandcorporation.com

SKYCHILD is the most emotionally immediate artist on the Wunderland Records roster — an indie-electronic project built around the feeling of being suspended between worlds. Their music exists in the liminal space between dream-pop and ambient electronic, where every song feels like a memory you can't quite place.

Signed in 2021 after a viral moment that saw their self-produced track accumulate millions of streams without any label backing, SKYCHILD brought an organic audience and a fully-formed artistic vision to Wunderland Records. The label's role has been to amplify, not to shape.

SKYCHILD's live shows are known for their immersive visual design — projected cloudscapes, spatial audio, and an intimacy that makes arenas feel like bedrooms.

“I write songs for people who feel too much and don't know what to do with it.”

— SKYCHILD
