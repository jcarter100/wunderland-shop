Extended profile · via wunderlandcorporation.com

MSBOZZYB is the most commercially powerful and artistically uncompromising voice in the Wunderland Records roster. An R&B and trap-soul artist whose music combines the emotional depth of neo-soul with the sonic ambition of contemporary pop, MSBOZZYB writes songs that feel like confessions dressed in couture.

Her music is defined by a rare combination of vulnerability and authority — she writes about love, power, identity, and ambition with equal conviction, and her vocal delivery carries the weight of every word. MSBOZZYB does not perform emotion; she transmits it.

Signed to Wunderland Records in 2024, MSBOZZYB has already established herself as one of the label's most important signings. Her debut album 'EMERALD' debuted at number one across multiple markets and earned her three award nominations in her first year.

“I built this from nothing. Now I build it into everything.”

— MSBOZZYB
