Extended profile · via wunderlandcorporation.com

JULIAN.EXE is a digital identity — a producer and artist who treats music as code, the studio as a terminal, and the listener as the system being executed. Their music is fast, precise, and deliberately disorienting: trap rhythms filtered through cyberpunk aesthetics, melodies that feel like corrupted data reassembling itself in real time.

The name is both a statement and a threat. JULIAN.EXE is not a person — it is a process. A program running on the hardware of contemporary culture, exploiting vulnerabilities in genre convention, overwriting the expected with the unexpected.

Signed to Wunderland Records in 2024 after a series of releases that circulated through underground tech communities and digital art spaces, JULIAN.EXE represents the label's expanding vision of what electronic music can be in the age of artificial intelligence and digital identity.

“I don't make music. I write programs that happen to sound like music.”

— JULIAN.EXE
